Which AI model does MITAI use?

A Random Forest classifier (scikit-learn, 100 decision trees) that reads DNA like text. It predicts which of 7 gene families a sequence belongs to — for example Tyrosine kinase, the family behind CML and HER2 breast cancer. The cancer links, mutation flags and risk score are then added by hand-written rules on top of that prediction.

How the model works
  1. K-mers: DNA has no spaces, so it is cut into overlapping 6-letter pieces. A sequence of length L gives L − 5 k-mers.
  2. Bag of words: CountVectorizer treats every run of 4 consecutive k-mers as one "phrase" and counts them — the same technique used for spam filters.
  3. Decision trees: each tree asks yes/no questions like "does this phrase appear?" until it reaches a family.
  4. Voting: 100 trees are each trained on a random sample of data and features, then vote. The share of votes becomes the confidence %.
  5. Training: 6,882 real gene sequences (human, chimpanzee, dog) from a public genomics dataset, saved as model.pkl.
Why a Random Forest?
  • Fits the data size: ~7,000 sequences is small; deep networks need far more.
  • Handles sparse features: each sequence uses a few thousand of 248,487 phrases, which trees handle well.
  • Robust: averaging 100 trees avoids the overfitting of a single tree.
  • Gives probabilities: vote shares give a confidence for all 7 families.
  • Cheap and explainable: trains in seconds on a laptop, no GPU — fits our low-cost goal.
Other models and their trade-offs
ModelTrade-off
Naive BayesVery fast, a classic baseline for k-mer counts; assumes features are independent
Support Vector MachineStrong on sparse text, but slower and gives no natural probabilities
CNN on raw DNALearns motifs itself, but needs much more data and a GPU
DNA / protein language models (DNABERT, ESM-2)State of the art and our planned next stage; ESM-2 needs DNA translated to protein first
Limits: the model predicts gene family, not cancer. The training data has no cancer labels, so cancer associations come from a literature-based lookup. Real detection would need labelled variant data such as ClinVar or COSMIC. See the Model Evaluation tab for honest accuracy figures.
